# Openfarms Lab

### Building practical technology for African agriculture.

Openfarms Lab is an early-stage agritech startup building accessible digital and AI-powered tools for African farmers.

We are working toward a simple goal: **help farmers make better decisions, reduce avoidable losses, and improve farm productivity using technology designed around their actual environment.**

Our work combines software, agricultural knowledge, farm data, and artificial intelligence to explore better ways of supporting farmers throughout the farming cycle.

---

## What We're Building

Our current focus is an **AI Extension Worker** for smallholder farmers.

Traditional agricultural information is often fragmented, difficult to access when needed, or too generic to account for an individual farmer's situation.

We are building toward an assistant that can understand context such as:

* Crop and variety
* Farm location
* Planting date
* Crop growth stage
* Previous farm activities
* Farmer observations
* Seasonal conditions

Instead of treating every farmer question as a new conversation, Openfarms aims to maintain a **persistent farm memory** that allows the system to provide increasingly relevant support throughout the season.

The goal is not simply an agricultural chatbot.

**We want to build a digital extension worker that knows the farm, remembers the season, and helps the farmer decide what to do next.**

---

## From Prototype to Focused Product

Openfarms started with an extensive prototype built during a hackathon.

The project allowed our team to explore several problems across the farming lifecycle and build working foundations across frontend, backend, and AI services.

After the hackathon, we evaluated seven potential Minimum Viable Features (MVFs) and selected the **AI Extension Worker** as the primary direction for our next stage of development.

The broader prototype remains valuable to our research and product roadmap, but our immediate focus is validating one core experience before expanding further.

---

## Our Approach

A farmer creates a farm profile containing basic information such as crop, location, planting date, and farm size.

As farming activities happen, Openfarms can build a history of the season.

When support is needed, our intended architecture combines:

**Farmer Context + Farm Memory + Trusted Agricultural Knowledge + AI Reasoning**

to provide guidance relevant to that farmer's situation.

Over time, other signals such as weather, crop observations, disease information, market information, and post-harvest data can contribute to the same farmer intelligence layer.

---

## Built for African Farming

Technology for African agriculture needs to account for the conditions in which farmers actually operate.

As Openfarms develops, we are designing around:

* Low-bandwidth environments
* Intermittent internet connectivity
* Mobile-first access
* Familiar communication channels
* Voice-based interaction
* African language support
* Locally relevant crops and farming practices
* Affordable AI infrastructure
* Simple interfaces that do not require technical expertise

We are starting with the realities of farmers in **Nigeria**, while building with the broader African agricultural context in mind.

---

## Where We're Going

Our current priority is building and validating the AI Extension Worker.

Our broader product research has also explored:

* Smart season planning and farm memory
* Crop monitoring and alerts
* Pest and disease support
* Agricultural outbreak intelligence
* Weather-informed recommendations
* Post-harvest support
* Market and price information
* Farmer-to-buyer connections

These represent possible future capabilities rather than features we claim to have fully deployed today.

Our approach is to **validate the core farmer problem first, learn from real usage, and expand based on evidence.**
